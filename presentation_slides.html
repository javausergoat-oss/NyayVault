The Problem & Motivation

Vulnerabilities in Current Legal Evidence Handling

1

Physical Evidence Tampering

  • • Paper FIRs, seizure memos, and case dockets suffer from loss, misplacement, and physical decay.
  • • High risk of document substitution or unauthorized physical alterations during transit between police stations and courts.
  • • Disputed forensic proof stalls trial proceedings.
CRITICAL RISK
2

Broken Chain of Custody

  • • No unified digital trail across Police, Forensics, Lawyers, and the Judiciary.
  • • Inability to provide proof of electronic custody under BSA Sec 63 / Sec 65B.
  • • Zero audit logging of who viewed, shared, or downloaded sensitive case records.
LEGAL ADMISSIBILITY ISSUE
3

Trial Delays & PII Leaks

  • • Judges spend hundreds of hours manually reading thousands of disorganized case pages.
  • • Unredacted victim and witness identities (Aadhaar, contact, address) leaked in public court records.
  • • Escalates India's backlog of 5+ Crore pending cases.
JUDICIAL BOTTLENECK
Our Solution

NyayVault: End-to-End Digital Evidence Ecosystem

🔒

Cryptographic Integrity & Live SHA-256

Every document is fingerprinted upon upload. The system runs real-time integrity verification on every retrieval, showing instantaneous VERIFIED_AUTHENTIC status and generating BSA Sec 63 certificates.

📁

Role-Partitioned Evidence Vault

Strict folder partitioning by legal role (Investigation, Forensic, Judicial, Prosecution, Defense, Registrar). Defense lawyers cannot access confidential case diaries until formally filed in court.

🤖

AI Document Intelligence & Redaction

Automated OCR text extraction, document classification with confidence metrics, and 1-click AI Redaction Studio to sanitize Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, and victim identities non-destructively.

🧠

RAG Case Assistant & Summarizer

Contextual AI copilot grounded exclusively in uploaded case evidence using vector embeddings (pgvector). Generates 1-page executive summaries, witness timelines, and spots contradictory statements.

Legal & Regulatory Standards

Full Compliance with New Indian Legal Reforms

Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023
Section 61 & 63 — Electronic Records Admissibility

Automates digital hash certificates (SHA-256) and complete custody trails, satisfying strict requirements for digital evidence submission in Indian courts without evidentiary dispute.

Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023
Section 173/193 (Charge Sheet) & Sec 489 (Bail)

Implements structured police-to-court electronic filing workflows, enabling instant electronic charge sheet verification and digital bail hearings.

Information Technology Act, 2000
Section 65B & Cyber Crime Protocols

Maintains immutable audit records tracking user ID, IP address, timestamp, and actions for forensic integrity during cyber fraud and digital crime trials.

Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023
Data Minimization & Witness Privacy

Integrated AI Redaction Studio sanitizes Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, and victim identifiers to prevent privacy violations in public court dockets.
