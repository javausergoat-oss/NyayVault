Details of Investigation:
Following the complaint filed by the victim regarding a cyber fraud of 5 Lakhs, our cyber cell tracked the IP address of the malicious application "QuickWealth". The IP address (192.168.45.12) resolved to a local proxy server in Delhi.

During the raid at the proxy location, we apprehended a suspect named "Vikram Singh". Upon confiscating his mobile device, we found multiple burner SIM cards. Call detail records (CDR) show frequent communications with a mastermind using the phone number: +91-9876543210.

The suspect's laptop is currently being sent to the Forensic Examiner (Dr. Anita Desai) for deep disk imaging. 

Note: The suspect has denied all allegations and claims his identity was stolen.
